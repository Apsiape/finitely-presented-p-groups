# Sources

The paper builds on two preprints of OpenAI's mathematics release, read at commit
`1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a` of [openai/math](https://github.com/openai/math):

- **[P]** "An infinite finitely presented periodic group" (23 September 2026),
  `preprints/An-infinite-finitely-presented-periodic-group-September-23-2026/`.
- **[F]** "An infinite finitely presented residually finite 2-group" (5 October 2026),
  `preprints/An-infinite-finitely-presented-residually-finite-2-group-October-5-2026/`.

Statement numbers of [P] and [F] cited in the paper were read from their PDFs at that commit. OpenAI's Lean
development (`lean/docs/247.md`) formalizes the characteristic-two periodic group of [P]; it formalizes nothing in
the present paper.

No OpenAI source text, code or Lean file is redistributed here.
